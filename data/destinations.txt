GOA
Goa is a coastal state in western India known for beautiful beaches.
Best time to visit: November to February when weather is cool and dry.
Popular beaches: Baga, Calangute, Anjuna, Palolem, Vagator.
Average budget: 8000-15000 rupees for 3 days including travel from Hyderabad.
Travel from Hyderabad: Flight 1.5 hours costs 3000-6000 rupees. Train 14 hours costs 800-1500 rupees.
Must eat: Fish curry rice, prawn balchao, bebinca dessert, feni drink.
Activities: Water sports, beach shacks, spice plantations, old Goa churches, night markets.
Weather December: 25-32 degrees celsius, sunny, perfect beach weather.

MANALI
Manali is a hill station in Himachal Pradesh in northern India.
Best time to visit: October to June for sightseeing, December to February for snow.
Popular spots: Rohtang Pass, Solang Valley, Old Manali, Hadimba Temple, Kasol nearby.
Average budget: 10000-18000 rupees for 4 days including travel from Hyderabad.
Travel from Hyderabad: Flight to Delhi then bus 14 hours. Total cost 4000-8000 rupees.
Must eat: Trout fish, Siddu bread, Dham traditional meal, Tibetan momos.
Activities: Skiing, paragliding, river rafting, trekking, snowfall watching.
Weather December: Minus 5 to 10 degrees celsius, heavy snowfall, carry warm clothes.

KERALA
Kerala is called Gods Own Country, located in southwest India.
Best time to visit: September to March, avoiding monsoon season.
Popular spots: Alleppey backwaters, Munnar tea gardens, Wayanad forests, Kovalam beach, Thekkady wildlife.
Average budget: 10000-20000 rupees for 5 days including travel from Hyderabad.
Travel from Hyderabad: Flight to Kochi 1.5 hours costs 2500-5000 rupees.
Must eat: Kerala fish curry, appam with stew, puttu kadala, prawn moilee, payasam.
Activities: Houseboat stay, elephant sanctuary, tea plantation walks, Kathakali dance, Ayurveda massage.
Weather December: 24-32 degrees celsius, light showers possible, mostly pleasant.

RAJASTHAN
Rajasthan is the land of kings, deserts and palaces in northwest India.
Best time to visit: October to March when desert heat is manageable.
Popular spots: Jaipur pink city, Udaipur lake city, Jodhpur blue city, Jaisalmer desert, Pushkar.
Average budget: 12000-25000 rupees for 5 days including travel from Hyderabad.
Travel from Hyderabad: Flight to Jaipur 2 hours costs 3000-7000 rupees.
Must eat: Dal baati churma, laal maas, ghewar sweet, pyaaz kachori, chai.
Activities: Camel safari, palace tours, desert camping, folk music evenings, shopping for handicrafts.
Weather December: 8-22 degrees celsius, cold nights, warm days, perfect sightseeing weather.

ANDAMAN ISLANDS
Andaman is a tropical island territory in the Bay of Bengal.
Best time to visit: October to May, clear waters perfect for diving.
Popular spots: Havelock Island, Neil Island, Cellular Jail, Radhanagar Beach, Ross Island.
Average budget: 20000-35000 rupees for 5 days including travel from Hyderabad.
Travel from Hyderabad: Flight to Port Blair 2.5 hours costs 5000-12000 rupees.
Must eat: Fresh seafood, coconut prawn curry, grilled lobster, banana flower dishes.
Activities: Scuba diving, snorkelling, sea walking, kayaking, glass bottom boat, jungle trekking.
Weather December: 23-30 degrees celsius, calm seas, excellent visibility for underwater activities.
